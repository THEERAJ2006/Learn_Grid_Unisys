LearnGrid Offline Getting Started

This is an offline-first learning app. Everything you see here is available without internet.

Try these MVP features:
1) Open a content item and scroll. LearnGrid tracks your progress locally.
2) Use Search. Keyword search is instant; Semantic search builds simple embeddings and ranks results.
3) In Content Viewer, tap Explain to get a short explanation. Offline mode uses a small rule-based summarizer.
4) If you later enable online AI (optional), your selected text may be sent to a cloud provider, only with consent.

Privacy notes:
- Progress, engagement signals, and AI cache are stored locally (Drift/SQLite).
- No API keys are bundled with the app. If you add keys in .env, they stay on-device.

Sample concept:
Spaced repetition is a study technique that schedules reviews over increasing intervals. It helps long-term retention by revisiting material right before you forget it.

Questions to ask:
- What is spaced repetition?
- Why does reviewing later help memory?
- How could you apply it to your own study plan?
